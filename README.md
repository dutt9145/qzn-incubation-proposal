## What QZN is

QZN is a working on-chain arcade for Qubic. Three games run at [qzn.app](https://qzn.app) today. All six smart contracts are deployed to testnet at indices 26–31 and have completed the constellation architecture refactor, with 406 procedure and invariant tests passing across the suite.

## What we need

**25B QU to ship to mainnet:**

- **20B for the audit** (mundus_tj85, paid in four tranches against milestones)
- **5B for operations** to remediate findings, integrate the token, and launch

## What the Board gets back

1. **7.5% of every protocol revenue distribution** until we pay back 10B QU (Component A cap) or 36 months pass — whichever first
2. **15M QZN tokens** (6.0% of total supply) on a 36-month linear vest, breaking even at 1,000 QU/QZN token price
